# PORTFOLIO REFERENCE COMPOSITION GOVERNANCE STANDARD

Status: **CANÔNICO · ADITIVO · OBRIGATÓRIO**  
Effective date: **2026-09-23**  
Scope: every new or materially redesigned `/portfolio/:slug` in 0WEB.

This standard formally consolidates the additions introduced after the deep audit
of public reference `https://ffix.com.br/` and its relevant public internal
routes. It **adds to** the existing 0WEB portfolio standards. It does not replace,
weaken or supersede identity, enrichment, media, funnel, SEO, accessibility,
performance, privacy, originality or quality rules.

## 1. Official decision

0WEB does **not** use external references as templates.

External references are decomposed into transferable design/decision grammar and
then reinterpreted through the real client, its offer, evidence, media, brand,
constraints and conversion goal.

Canonical principle:

> **ABSORB GRAMMAR — NEVER COPY SILHOUETTE.**

The system must never produce:

`known template + client name + new colors + new text + new image = new site`.

A different client requires a different perceptual and decision experience.

## 2. Why this standard exists

The recurring failure observed in recent portfolio work was not lack of color,
copy or visual polish. The failure was **structural sameness**:

- same hero geometry;
- same section order;
- same image/text ratio;
- same card family;
- same CTA distribution;
- same proof placement;
- same mobile collapse;
- same motion grammar;
- same closing structure;
- same decision journey.

Changing logo, palette, copy and assets does not solve this.

The acceptance test remains:

> Remove logo, name, colors, copy and photos. If two projects still have the
> same silhouette/journey, the result is not exclusive enough.

## 3. FFIX audit — principles officially absorbed

The FFIX reference was studied as a **decision/composition specimen**, never as a
layout source file.

Observed transferable principles:

| Context | Structural behavior observed | Principle adopted by 0WEB |
|---|---|---|
| Multi-offer home | quote/configuration appears early; services, workshop, used inventory, process, locality, diagnostic utilities, proof and education receive different visual weights | mix commercial, utility, editorial and proof chapters instead of repeating one block family |
| Specific service pages | problem-specific explanation and service taxonomy change the early narrative | the offer must be allowed to reshape the page |
| Used-device inventory | inventory/browsing dominates before reassurance and trade/sell flows | product intent requires a product-led journey, not a service skeleton |
| TV repair | “repair or replace?” becomes a decision aid | important uncertainty may become a tool/module at the point of doubt |
| Strong guarantee offer | guarantee rises in hierarchy | a real differentiator may reorder the composition |
| Notebook/service limitations | services not performed are made explicit | material scope limits should qualify the visitor before conversion |
| Inventory/availability | stock is treated as variable | volatile decision data must have freshness/provenance |
| Contact/status/diagnosis | multiple utilities support the user’s real task | interaction should solve the business-specific decision, not just decorate the page |

Media principle adopted:

> **Media has a narrative job.**

Real workshop imagery may prove craft; product photography may dominate an
inventory experience; location imagery may prove physical presence; process
media may prove execution; contextual/editorial art may create atmosphere when
it is not presented as factual evidence.

No rule authorizes copying FFIX branding, copy, assets, exact section sequence,
dimensions, proprietary interactions or final silhouette.

## 4. Mandatory matrices before layout

When a reference URL is supplied, or when a substantial new portfolio is being
designed, the creative process must evaluate the following matrices before final
JSX/composition.

### 4.1 ROLE_MATRIX

For each meaningful role:

`role | visitorQuestion | decisionJob | priority | evidence | conversionEffect`

Typical roles include:

- orientation;
- offer discovery;
- quick conversion;
- product/inventory browsing;
- value demonstration;
- risk reduction;
- decision support;
- proof;
- locality/feasibility;
- process;
- education;
- post-action continuity.

A role is **not a mandatory section**.

### 4.2 COMPOSITION_MATRIX

For each selected role:

`spatialTreatment | mediaTreatment | textMediaRatio | density | containment |
overlap/layering | interaction | mobileTranslation`

Purpose: force explicit reasoning about geometry/silhouette instead of defaulting
to centered container + repeated cards.

### 4.3 RHYTHM_MEDIA_MATRIX

Map the page as perceptual states:

`density | mediaWeight | containment | contrast | interaction | proofLevel`

Examples:

- `AIRY / DENSE`
- `MEDIA_DOMINANT / TEXT_DOMINANT / BALANCED`
- `FULL_BLEED / CONTAINED / OVERLAPPED`
- `QUIET / CONTRASTED`
- `STATIC / INTERACTIVE`

The final project must derive its own cadence.

### 4.4 ANTI_COPY_MATRIX

Every audited reference produces three explicit outputs:

1. **transferable principle**;
2. **project-specific translation**;
3. **do not copy**.

The third field must cover exact composition, section order, visual identity,
copy, assets, proprietary interaction, dimensions/measurements and any
brand-specific device.

### 4.5 SCOPE_TRUTH_MATRIX

When material to the decision, record:

`supported | notSupported | conditional | requiresDiagnosis | geography |
availabilityDependency | evidence | customerImpact | placement`

This prevents the page from implying a wider capability than the business
actually has.

### 4.6 FRESHNESS_MATRIX

For volatile decision data:

`field | volatility | source | verifiedAt | staleAfter | refreshStrategy |
fallbackWhenStale | owner`

Applicable examples include:

- price;
- stock;
- availability;
- schedule;
- deadline;
- promotion;
- rating/review count;
- opening hours;
- coverage conditions.

Recommended states:

- `STATIC_VERIFIED`
- `DYNAMIC_VERIFIED`
- `STALE_BLOCKED`
- `ASK_FOR_CURRENT_AVAILABILITY`
- `HIDE_UNTIL_REFRESHED`

## 5. Composition fingerprint — expanded official view

The project composition fingerprint is no longer limited to visible geometry.

Existing fields remain relevant:

`heroGeometry | headerTreatment | sectionGraph | contentOrder | gridTopology |
mediaDistribution | backgroundRhythm | proofPlacement | ctaDistribution |
navigationPattern | motionSignature | closingStructure`

The governance model also requires evaluation of:

`roleGraph | mediaCadence | interactionLoci | decisionAidPlacement |
densityRhythm | mobileCompositionStrategy`

A page can therefore fail originality even if its colors/assets are unique.

## 6. Media composition repertoire

The following are capabilities, **not presets**:

- editorial singular image;
- full-bleed photography;
- masked/cropped/layered media;
- asymmetric collage;
- mosaic/masonry;
- product shelf/inventory;
- horizontal media rail;
- image crossing chapters;
- process media beside the step it proves;
- proof media adjacent to the claim it supports;
- gallery/lightbox when exploration is useful;
- physical-location media as locality/craft evidence;
- poster/video when movement communicates better than a still;
- graphic/SVG/editorial composition when factual photography is not appropriate.

Anti-pattern:

`same 4:3 image card + title + paragraph` repeated as the dominant visual
language across different clients.

## 7. Decision-specific functionality

A unique landing may require a business-specific decision aid.

Examples:

- comparison;
- selector;
- configurator;
- coverage checker;
- diagnostic;
- availability inquiry;
- inventory filter;
- estimate/calculator;
- appointment path;
- product/category browsing;
- “repair or replace?” support.

No widget is mandatory. The mandatory step is deciding whether a specific user
uncertainty deserves functionality instead of another generic section.

## 8. New canonical blockers

The following states are formal quality failures when applicable:

### Reference/composition

- `REFERENCE_DECOMPOSITION_MISSING`
- `REFERENCE_USED_AS_TEMPLATE`
- `SAME_JOURNEY_DIFFERENT_COPY`
- `SECTION_ROLE_MONOTONY`
- `MEDIA_RHYTHM_REUSED`
- `FUNCTIONAL_DIVERSITY_MISSING`

### Truth/freshness

- `SCOPE_OVERCLAIM`
- `MATERIAL_LIMIT_HIDDEN`
- `STALE_DECISION_DATA`
- `FAKE_LIVE_AVAILABILITY`
- `VOLATILE_CLAIM_WITHOUT_SOURCE`

These add to existing blockers such as `GENERIC_COMPOSITION`,
`SHARED_VISUAL_SKELETON`, `SKIN_SWAP`, `DEFAULT_SECTION_ORDER`,
`REPEATED_GRID_TOPOLOGY`, `REPEATED_MOTION_GRAMMAR`,
`NO_SIGNATURE_MOMENT`, `MEDIA_STARVATION`,
`NO_BRAND_TEST_FAIL`, `HIGH_SIMILARITY_UNRESOLVED` and
`VISUAL_QA_MISSING`.

## 11. Enforcement status

### Already enforced / operational

- mandatory routing through portfolio art direction and zero-generic policy;
- machine-readable policy contains the new requirements/blockers;
- quality documentation treats the new failures as blockers;
- reference decomposition is part of the required pre-layout process;
- truth/freshness review is part of the required research/creative process;
- standard CI validates that these governance changes do not regress the portal.

### Not yet fully automated as a structural similarity algorithm

The current executable `PROJECT_UNIQUENESS_GATE` still computes similarity from
its existing fingerprint fields. The new fields `roleGraph`, `mediaCadence`,
`interactionLoci`, `decisionAidPlacement`, `densityRhythm` and
`mobileCompositionStrategy` are **normative and required by governance**, but
are not yet all scored numerically by the existing similarity algorithm.

Likewise, `SCOPE_TRUTH_MATRIX` and `FRESHNESS_MATRIX` are now canonical
requirements, but a dedicated executable validator for all of their fields has
not yet been introduced.

This distinction is intentional: the new standards were added without
retroactively breaking all published projects.

## 12. Safe next technical hardening

The remaining engineering improvement is to introduce a versioned contract for
**new projects only**, so executable gates can validate:

- the six new experience-fingerprint fields;
- presence/completeness of scope truth when applicable;
- presence/completeness of freshness metadata for volatile claims;
- stale-data behavior;
- reference-decomposition evidence when a reference URL was supplied.

The rollout must be version-gated so legacy projects do not fail simply because
they predate this standard.

## 13.1 Reference corpus program — multi-source, never single-template

The FFIX audit remains a foundational example, but reference research is now a
**versioned corpus**, not a single-site doctrine.

Canonical sources:

- `docs/research/PORTFOLIO_REFERENCE_CORPUS_R1_2026-09-23.md`;
- `src/config/portfolio-reference-pattern-library.json`.

For new projects, reference selection must be based on the visitor/business
decision mode, not merely the client's segment.

Required qualitative inputs:

`offerMode | decisionVelocity | decisionRisk | physicalProofStrength |
mediaSupply | locationComplexity | catalogComplexity | humanTrustNeed |
diagnosticNeed | priceTransparency | recurringOffer | b2bNeed |
productDemoNeed`

Prefer **2–4 complementary references** when useful. One source must never become
the visual master template.

### Reference use class

Every extracted signal receives:

- `USE` — directly transferable principle;
- `ADAPT` — useful after contextual/factual/technical translation;
- `REJECT` — anti-pattern; never reproduce.

A visually attractive site can still contribute `REJECT` signals such as
unverified reviews, zero-state counters, stale pricing or generic testimonials.

### Interaction vs motion evidence

Reference research must distinguish:

`interactionObserved` from `motionObserved`.

HTML/crawl evidence may confirm selectors, forms, tabs, carousels, multi-step
flows and stateful controls. It does **not** prove easing, timing, parallax,
clip-path choreography or microanimation.

For motion research use:

`source | observationMode | interactionObserved | motionObserved |
derivedMotionCandidate | narrativeJob | implementationPrimitive |
reducedMotionFallback | confidence`

When no browser/render evidence exists, `motionObserved = NOT_VERIFIED`.
Derived motion ideas remain 0WEB creative proposals, not claims about the source.




## 14. Final rule

A client must not receive a site that looks like a recolored member of the same
template family.

The expected result is:

`shared engineering + client truth + client media + client decision journey +
client-specific composition + client-specific interaction + honest constraints
= independent commercial site`.
